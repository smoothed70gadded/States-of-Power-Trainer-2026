## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **States of Power**. This trainer provides optional single-player assistance for managing national resources, military production, research, manpower, construction, and game speed. It is designed around States of Power's economy, technology, diplomacy, province development, and direct division-command systems.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to experiment with alternative World War II strategies, test economic and military systems, create unusual historical outcomes, or reduce campaign difficulty in single-player games.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

- **Unlimited Construction Resources** — Prevents selected construction and development resources from being depleted while building factories, research labs, ports, and infrastructure.

- **Unlimited Money** — Provides a stable financial reserve for economic management, loans, trade, and national development.
- **Game Speed Control** — Allows the player to pause or adjust the pace of the real-time campaign for planning and execution.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Enhanced Army Strength** — Improves the effectiveness and endurance of selected divisions during battles.
- **No Supply Pressure** — Reduces the impact of supply-line limitations on supported divisions during extended operations.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

States of Power lets you command divisions directly across the strategic map in real time with pause controls. Select a division, issue its movement order, and monitor frontlines and supply routes. Use the trainer's speed controls to pause while organizing armies or slow the campaign when planning large operations.

### Tools & Inventory

The game does not use a character inventory system. Instead, manage national stockpiles, military supplies, money, goods, manpower, and construction capacity through the economy and province-management interfaces. Resource options in the trainer are intended to support these strategic reserves.

### World & Environment

Develop provinces with factories, research labs, ports, and infrastructure. Manage construction, trade, loans, technology, manpower, and national ideology while negotiating with other countries. The trainer is most useful when testing custom national builds, alternate-history campaigns, and scenarios created with the built-in Scenario Editor.

### Combat & Weapons

Build armies from infantry, tanks, artillery, fighter planes, and other available unit types. Each unit performs differently against opposing forces, so composition, positioning, morale, and supply remain important. Use combat options to test division designs and offensive plans, while avoiding trainer use in multiplayer.
